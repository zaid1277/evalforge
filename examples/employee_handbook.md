# Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook

*This is a fictional handbook for a fictional company, created only to
demonstrate EvalForge. It does not describe any real organization or
real employment terms.*

## Welcome

Northwind Analytics is a fictional data-consulting company with
approximately 120 employees. This handbook describes the policies that
apply to all full-time employees. It is provided to every new hire on
their first day and is reviewed annually by the People team.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, accrued
monthly from the employee's start date. Leave requests must be submitted
through the HR portal at least five business days in advance for
requests of three days or longer.

Employees with five or more years of continuous service receive an
additional five days of annual leave per year, for a total of 30 days.
Unused annual leave may be carried over into the following year, up to a
maximum of five days; any leave beyond that cap is forfeited at the end
of the calendar year unless local law requires otherwise.

Public holidays are granted separately from annual leave and follow the
standard holiday calendar published by the People team each January.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to team
manager approval. Remote work arrangements are reviewed quarterly and
may be adjusted based on team needs. Employees working remotely are
expected to be reachable during their team's core collaboration hours
(see Working Hours below) and to attend any in-person meetings their
manager designates as mandatory.

Fully remote arrangements (five days per week) are only available to
employees in roles explicitly designated as remote-first by their
department head, and must be documented in writing.

## Working Hours

The standard work week is 37.5 hours. Core collaboration hours, during
which all employees are expected to be available for meetings and quick
responses, are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.
Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedule flexibly
with their manager's agreement, provided their total weekly hours are
met.

Overtime is not routinely expected. Employees who anticipate working
more than 45 hours in a single week should discuss this with their
manager in advance.

## Equipment

New employees receive a company laptop and a standard peripheral kit
(monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset) before their first day. Laptops
are refreshed on a three-year cycle or sooner if a device develops a
hardware fault that support cannot repair.

Employees working from home may request a one-time home office setup
allowance of up to $300 to cover items such as a desk or chair. Requests
are submitted through the expense system (see Expenses below) with a
brief description of the items purchased.

All company equipment remains the property of Northwind Analytics and
must be returned within ten business days of an employee's last day of
employment.

## Probation

New employees serve a probationary period of 90 days from their start
date. During probation, either the employee or Northwind Analytics may
end the employment relationship with two weeks' written notice.

Performance and fit are reviewed with the employee's manager at the
30-day and 75-day marks. A successful 75-day review typically results in
confirmation of permanent employment at the end of the 90-day period;
the People team will confirm this in writing.

Annual leave continues to accrue during probation and may be used with
manager approval, though new hires are encouraged to avoid extended
leave in their first 90 days where possible.

## Expenses

Business-related expenses (client travel, approved software
subscriptions, conference attendance, and the home office allowance
described above) are submitted through the expense system within 30
days of the expense being incurred. Each submission requires an
itemized receipt.

Expense claims under $50 are auto-approved. Claims of $50 or more require
manager approval before reimbursement is processed. Reimbursements are
paid together with the employee's next regular payroll run following
approval.

## Training

Every employee has an annual training budget of $1,000 to spend on
courses, certifications, or conferences relevant to their role, subject
to manager approval. Unused training budget does not carry over to the
following year.

New employees complete a mandatory onboarding curriculum covering data
handling practices, security awareness, and internal tooling within
their first two weeks. Records of completed training are kept by the
People team.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and
December. Each review is a conversation between the employee and their
manager covering progress against goals set at the start of the review
period, along with goals for the next six months. Compensation changes,
when they occur, are communicated separately by the People team within
four weeks of the December review.

New employees are not included in the review cycle until they have
completed probation; their first formal review takes place at the next
scheduled cycle after their 90-day probationary period ends.

## Communication Tools

Northwind Analytics uses a company-wide chat tool for day-to-day
communication and a separate video conferencing tool for meetings.
Employees are expected to keep their status indicator up to date during
working hours so teammates in other time zones know when they are
available. Client-facing communication (email, shared documents, and
meeting invitations sent to clients) must use the employee's company
account, never a personal account.

## Health and Wellness

Northwind Analytics provides an annual wellness stipend of $200, which
employees may use toward gym memberships, fitness classes, or similar
wellness expenses. The stipend is claimed through the expense system
described above and, like other expense claims, requires a receipt.

Employees experiencing a workplace concern related to health or safety
should raise it with their manager or the People team as soon as
practical so it can be addressed promptly.

## Questions

Employees with questions about any policy in this handbook should
contact the People team. This handbook may be updated from time to
time; the version referenced here reflects the policies in effect as of
the most recent annual review.
